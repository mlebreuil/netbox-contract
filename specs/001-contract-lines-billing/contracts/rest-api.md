# Contract: REST API changes

Base path: `/api/plugins/contracts/` (existing). All endpoints follow the NetBox plugin API conventions already used by the plugin (token authentication, object permissions, `brief` mode, filtering, pagination, bulk operations). Single release, plugin 2.5.0.

## New endpoints

| Endpoint | Methods | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| `units/` | GET, POST, PATCH, PUT, DELETE | fields: `id`, `url`, `display`, `name`, `description`, `billing_method` (`one_time` / `recurring` / `usage`), `months`, `comments`, `tags`, `custom_fields`, `created`, `last_updated`. DELETE is refused (409) while contract lines or invoice lines use the unit. `months` required for `recurring`, forbidden otherwise. Changing `billing_method` or `months` is a 400 while a line of an invoiced contract, or an invoice line, uses the unit. |
| `contract-lines/` | GET, POST, PATCH, PUT, DELETE | fields: `contract` (id), `description`, `quantity`, `unit_price`, `unit` (id), `currency`, `start_date`, `end_date`, `accounting_dimensions` (ids), `comments`, `tags`, `custom_fields`, read-only `total_value`, `yearly_value`, `replaces` (the line replaced by an amendment), `invoiced_at_conversion`. `currency` defaults to the contract's; another value is a 400 naming both currencies. Dates outside the contract's dates are a 400. Creating or deleting a line of a contract that already has an invoice, or changing its contract terms (contract, description, quantity, unit price, unit, currency, dates, custom fields), is a 400 ("a new contract must be created"); its `accounting_dimensions`, `comments` and `tags` can still change. Filters: `contract_id`, `unit_id`, `unit`, `currency`, `billing_method`, `accounting_dimensions`, `invoice_id`, `q`. |
| `contract-lines/{id}/amend/` | POST | amends the unit price and/or quantity of a recurring or usage-based line from a date (FR-030): body `effective_date`, `reason` (required), `unit_price` and/or `quantity`. The line ends the day before `effective_date` and the new line that replaces it is returned (201). 400 when the date is not after the last invoiced period of the line or outside its dates, for a one-time line, a line already replaced, a missing reason or no change. Requires the add and change contract line permissions. |

## Changed endpoints

| Endpoint | Change |
|---|---|
| `contracts/` | new writable `billable` (default true); new read-only `total_contract_value` (null when not available), `yearly_contract_value`, `yearly_billable_value`; `mrc`, `yrc`, `nrc` stay, documented as deprecated (`calculated_rc` was never an API field, decision I8). The values are computed for a whole page of the list at once. Setting `currency` on a contract that has invoices or invoice lines is a 400. Setting `parent` to a contract of another currency, for a non-billable child, is a 400. Changing `billable` is a 400 when the contract or its family has invoices (FR-008a). Filter `billable`. |
| `invoices/` | `status` defaults to `draft` (FR-031). 400 when a new invoice lists more than one contract, or when its `currency` differs from its contract's; an existing invoice with several contracts can still be updated without adding contracts. A Posted invoice cannot change its `amount`, `currency`, `period_start`, `period_end` or `contracts` (400); its `status` can. Creating an invoice generates its lines (below) and no longer copies template lines. |
| `invoice-lines/` | new optional `contract_line` (id), `unit` (id), `unit_price` and `quantity`; `unit` and `unit_price` default to those of the contract line (FR-024, decision I11). When the line has a unit price, `amount` is calculated and ignored if sent; otherwise `amount` is required as today. 400 when `currency` differs from the invoice's or when the contract line does not belong to the invoice's contract or one of its non-billable descendants. Lines of a Posted invoice cannot be created or deleted, and their `unit`, `unit_price`, `quantity`, `amount`, `currency`, `contract_line` and `invoice` cannot change (400); their `accounting_dimensions`, `comments` and `tags` can (FR-031). |

## Invoice creation generates the lines

There is no separate generation endpoint. `POST invoices/` for a new invoice linked to one billable contract creates, in the same request, one invoice line per applicable contract line (its own lines plus those of non-billable descendants, stopping at billable children), each with `contract_line`, `unit`, `unit_price`, dimensions, currency, quantity and calculated amount set; usage-based lines get no quantity and amount 0 until the client sets it with `PATCH invoice-lines/{id}/`. It is a 400 to create an invoice for a non-billable contract, or one whose `amount` is lower than the total of the lines that would be generated (both amounts are in the message). Editing an invoice (`PATCH`/`PUT`) never generates lines, and neither does bulk import through the UI. The API always generates the default lines: changing quantities, unit prices and dimensions before creation, and adding lines without contract line, is offered by the new invoice screen only (FR-032).

## Compatibility

No field is removed or renamed; added fields are optional on write, except that invoice lines with a unit price get their amount calculated. The new Draft default of invoices and the Posted lock are behaviour changes listed in the changelog. Error messages are plain strings under the field name or `non_field_errors`, as elsewhere in the plugin.
