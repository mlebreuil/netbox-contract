## Verdict & Rationale

Go with Option C. The gate requires an adequate problem, adequate evidence and a recommended option, and all three are met: the problem is real in the code, the evidence is adequate overall even though demand beyond the maintainer is unproven, and the maintainer chose the phased option on 2026-09-26. Two `unknown` scores are acknowledged and not glossed over: the value against inaction is unquantified, and formal strategic fit is unknown because the constitution is empty. The go is conditional in practice on the carried-forward questions below being settled during specification, especially the tests on NetBox 4.6 and the rule for invoices with several contracts.

## If go — Handoff to `/speckit-specify`

- **Problem**: users of the netbox-contract plugin can describe a contract's cost only as one recurring and one non-recurring amount, so contracts with several billable contract lines, or with one-time and usage-based charges, cannot be represented accurately; their invoices are built and corrected by hand, and nothing keeps currencies consistent between a contract, its invoices and their lines.
- **Chosen approach**: Option C, phased. Release 1: contract lines and units (nature of cost, months covered per unit price, contract line dates, several accounting dimensions), the billable flag (True for all existing contracts), computed contract values (total, yearly, yearly billable), currency validation on save with a report of existing mismatches, the rule that a non-billable child has the same currency as its parent, migrations converting invoice templates and mrc/yrc/nrc into contract lines (old templates and fields kept, deprecated and hidden), `min_version` raised to 4.6.0 with no `max_version`, the CI pinned to a NetBox 4.6 tag, amounts stored with plain Django decimal and currency fields (django-money dropped, no money library, confirmed 2026-09-26), and the invoice pre-fill switched to the contract lines, computing the invoice amount and period from them with the recurring, proration-by-days and one-time rules (it no longer reads the deprecated cost fields; maintainer decision and release split confirmed 2026-09-26). Release 2: invoice generation from contract lines, adding usage handling and the child roll-up, a reference from each invoice line to its contract line, and retirement of templates from generation.
- **Decided billing rules (the recurring, proration and one-time rules apply from release 1 to the pre-fill amount; usage handling, child roll-up and invoice-line generation from release 2)**: a recurring unit's "month" is the months covered by one unit price (monthly 1, quarterly 3, yearly 12) and an invoice covering N months bills quantity x price x N / month; partial periods are prorated by days; a one-time cost's remaining amount is quantity x price minus the amounts already on Posted invoices for that contract line (Draft and Canceled ignored); usage-based lines start with the contract line's quantity and price and the user edits the quantity per invoice; all generated lines stay adjustable; a billable parent's invoice includes the contract lines of all non-billable descendants, stopping at any billable child; generation on a non-billable contract is an error.
- **In scope / out of scope**: in scope as above. Out of scope: NetBox 4.7 compatibility and replacing the custom-script mechanism, NetBox versions before 4.6, currency conversion or exchange rates, tax or VAT display (issue #177), automatic scheduled invoice generation, and separate usage records or metering. New code avoids the NetBox APIs removed in 4.7.
- **Success metrics**: a contract with several contract lines of different natures can be described without workarounds; prepared invoice amounts match reference scenarios for one-time, recurring and usage-based costs (scenarios to be written); mismatching currencies can no longer be saved; total, yearly and yearly billable values are shown; existing contracts, invoices and templates remain usable after upgrade; the existing tests are updated, new tests cover all the use cases (including the migrations), and all pass on NetBox 4.6.
- **Tests (to be part of the specification)**: modify the existing tests (views, imports, API, the query-count baseline) for the changed models, fields and pre-fill, and add new tests covering all use cases: each nature of cost (one-time, recurring, usage-based), months covered per unit, proration by days, the remaining amount on Posted invoices only, currency validation on save and the mismatch report, the parent-child currency rule, the billable flag and its default, the computed contract values, the conversion migrations for invoice templates and for mrc/yrc/nrc, and the pre-fill of release 1 (and, for release 2, invoice line generation, the child roll-up and the reference to the contract line). All must run on NetBox 4.6 with the CI pinned to a 4.6 tag.
- **Decided during specification (2026-09-26)**: new invoices link at most one contract and existing multi-contract invoices are left as they are; the three computed contract values follow the definitions in problem.md (Clarifications). The specification is `specs/001-contract-lines-billing/spec.md`.
